# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all, regardless of level of experience, gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, nationality, or other similar characteristic.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Being patient and understanding with beginners
- Helping others learn and grow

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxing) without explicit permission
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Discrimination based on protected characteristics
- Sustained disruption of discussions or activities

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have other concerns, please report it by:

- **Email**: maintainer@example.com
- **Private Message**: On GitHub to the project maintainers
- **Report**: Using GitHub's built-in reporting features

When reporting, please include:

- Your contact information
- Names of those involved (if any)
- Your account/contact and theirs (if different)
- Description of incident
- Relevant screenshots or evidence (if applicable)
- Any additional context

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly. The privacy and safety of report authors will be respected.

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

1. **Warning**: For minor violations, a private warning
2. **Temporary ban**: For more serious violations (24 hours to 7 days)
3. **Permanent ban**: For repeated violations or severe misconduct

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces including:

- GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Code reviews and comments
- Commit messages
- Documentation
- Any other project-related communications

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Exceptions

Disagreements and debates about approach are encouraged and expected. Criticism of ideas is not harassment. However, it must remain respectful and constructive.
